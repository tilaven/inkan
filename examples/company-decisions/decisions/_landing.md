## How we decide
Open an **RFC** while the answer is still open. Review it, set a `reviewBy` date, then record the outcome in an **ADR**. Only `accepted` and `implemented` records describe how we work today.

- Everything is Markdown in the repository, next to the code.
- One folder per team. A new team is a new folder.
- Superseded decisions stay in the archive and say what replaced them.
